# Following on the Fediverse (ActivityPub)

Each organisation has a Fediverse actor you can follow from Mastodon, Pleroma, or any ActivityPub-compatible platform.

The handle is shown on each organisation's page:

```
@{actor}@{domain}
```

Search for the handle in your Fediverse client and click Follow. New events will appear in your home feed as they are published.

You can also follow the shared relay actor to receive events from all organisations at once — its handle is shown on the organisations list page.
