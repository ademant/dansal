City search
Place names and postcodes of these countries (from GeoNames, CC BY 4.0) suggest towns while visitors type in the /search city filter — place names with typo tolerance, postcodes as an exact prefix match. Saving downloads and imports newly added countries in the background (a few MB each); removing a country deletes its places and postcodes. Not every country has a GeoNames postcode dump — "none" below just means this one doesn't, place search still works. Leave the field blank to disable — then only towns with events are suggested, and other places are looked up on Enter.
